= 1. Draw the protection boundary

== Show
At rest, in transit, and in use protect different parts of Northstar's claim data.

== Learn
Intel TDX can isolate a trust domain from host software, but capability does not
prove this workload ran there or matched an approved measurement.

== Do
[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
export TRUST_URL=http://sovereign-ai-301:8080
curl -fsS "$TRUST_URL/healthz" | python3 -m json.tool
----
[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
curl -fsS "$TRUST_URL/api/v1/scenarios" | tee /tmp/northstar-301-scenarios.json | python3 -m json.tool
----

== Prove
The service is `REHEARSAL` and `liveTdxObserved` is false. Residual risks and
unavailable observations stay visible.
