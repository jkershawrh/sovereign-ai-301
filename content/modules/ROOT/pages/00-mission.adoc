= 0. Accept the Northstar mission

== Show

Northstar's claims assistant processes regulated customer data. The business
owner wants confidential inference, the security owner requires fail-closed
verification, and the platform owner must be able to operate and reclaim the
environment without receiving authority to release protected resources.

== Learn

Sovereignty is a chain of accountable decisions, not a location checkbox.
Hardware capability, workload identity, evidence appraisal, resource policy,
inference authorization, and human approval must remain independently visible.

== Do

Create the qualification package you will complete throughout the lab.

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
export TRUST_URL=http://sovereign-ai-301:8080
export NORTHSTAR_DIR="$HOME/northstar-sovereign-301"
mkdir -p "$NORTHSTAR_DIR"
cat > "$NORTHSTAR_DIR/decision-brief.md" <<'EOF'
# Northstar sovereign inference qualification

- Customer outcome: protect regulated claim data while it is processed
- Data boundary: pending
- Workload identity: pending
- Evidence appraisal: pending
- Protected resource: pending
- Inference authority: not granted by this lab
- Failure posture: pending
- Human recommendation: pending
- Source state: REHEARSAL
EOF
----

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
curl -fsS "$TRUST_URL/healthz" | tee "$NORTHSTAR_DIR/service-health.json" | python3 -m json.tool
grep -E 'Customer outcome|Source state' "$NORTHSTAR_DIR/decision-brief.md"
----

== Prove

The service reports `REHEARSAL`, and your decision brief states both the customer
outcome and the evidence boundary before any qualification request is made.
